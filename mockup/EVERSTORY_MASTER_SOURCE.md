# Everstory Studio Mockup Master Source

**Version:** 1.0  
**Updated:** 2026-07-29  
**Current production state:** Concept Mockup Mode — actual product reference images not yet registered

## 1. Purpose

This document is the standalone source of truth for Everstory Studio mockup planning. It combines only the business, product, visual, and merchandising facts required for image production.

The immediate goal is to establish an Everstory-consistent visual system before actual product photography is available. The long-term goal is to replace provisional product representations with real product references without rebuilding approved scenes.

## 2. Business Definition

Everstory Studio is a small Toronto-based custom photo sticker studio.

Customer photos are manually refined, color-checked, printed, laminated, precision-cut, and packed in Toronto. The customer promise is not mass automation; it is careful handling of personal photographs and a finished object made to keep.

### Positioning

- personal keepsake rather than novelty merchandise,
- human-refined edges rather than generic automatic masking,
- Korean premium substrates,
- in-house production workflow,
- local Toronto craft with Canada-wide fulfillment,
- warm, restrained, editorial presentation.

### Core audience

- Toronto GTA and Canadian custom-gift customers,
- Korean diaspora,
- families,
- pet owners,
- couples,
- planner and journal users,
- customers looking for a small personal gift or repeat purchase.

### Brand language

- `Photographs, kept with care.`
- `Photographs, kept by hand.`
- `Made to keep.`
- `Moments that matter.`
- A sticker becomes a keepsake through care.

Do not fill mockups with marketing copy. These phrases describe the emotional direction unless the user requests a designed ad asset.

## 3. Product Truth

### Core format

- ISO A5 sticker sheet: 148 × 210 mm.
- Custom photo die-cut stickers made from customer-supplied images.
- Sticker size is measured along the longest edge.
- Sticker count changes with photo proportion, selected size, SKU, and sheet layout.
- A single universal sticker count must not be shown or claimed.
- All products are laminated.

### Launch SKUs

| SKU | Definition | Confirmed output |
|---|---|---|
| Face Sticker | Face or upper-body-focused die-cut photo stickers | Sheet count and sticker count depend on selections |
| Full Body Sticker | Full person, pet, or subject die-cut photo stickers | Sheet count and sticker count depend on selections |
| Shape Sticker | Shape-cropped photo stickers | Round crop at launch |
| Package Mini | Studio-curated multi-photo package | 4 selected photos, 1 A5 sheet |
| Package Full | Studio-curated multi-photo package | 8 selected photos, 2 A5 sheets |

### Non-package size options

- 0.75 in / 19 mm
- 1 in / 25 mm
- 1.25 in / 32 mm
- 1.5 in / 38 mm
- 2 in / 51 mm
- 2.5 in / 64 mm
- Mixed

Do not infer the visible sticker count from a size without an actual production layout reference.

### Materials

| Material | Visual intent | Mockup restriction |
|---|---|---|
| White Matte | neutral, soft photographic base | Can be approximated only in Concept Mode |
| Translucent | layered look on glass or light surfaces | Requires actual reference for product-accurate rendering |
| Silver | reflective waterproof accent | Requires actual reference for product-accurate rendering |
| Gold | warm reflective waterproof accent | Requires actual reference for product-accurate rendering |

All four use lamination. Do not invent exact gloss, reflectivity, texture, thickness, or transparency without an actual product image.

## 4. Product Image Status

### Current status

No approved actual product reference is registered in this source package.

Therefore:

- every generated sheet is provisional,
- every generated sticker arrangement is provisional,
- synthetic people may be used only as non-identifiable placeholders,
- mockups may establish style and composition,
- mockups may not serve as print, cut, color, material, or quality proof.

### Concept Mockup label

Use the internal status:

```text
Concept Mockup — scene and style exploration; product artwork is provisional.
```

This label does not need to appear inside the generated image unless requested. It must appear in the production brief and QA record.

### Replacement-friendly requirements

- Keep the complete A5 sheet visible whenever possible.
- Avoid severe perspective distortion.
- Avoid fingers covering important sheet areas.
- Avoid props crossing over the product.
- Avoid patterned shadows on the product.
- Keep a clear visual boundary between product and background.
- Prefer top-down or near-front angles.
- Preserve sufficient resolution for later image editing.

## 5. Visual Direction

### Central phrase

**Warm editorial keepsake**

### Desired qualities

- calm and human,
- warm but not yellow,
- premium but approachable,
- tactile but clean,
- minimal but not sterile,
- emotionally personal without sentimental clichés,
- realistic ecommerce photography.

### Brand palette

| Token | Value | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Ink | `#000000` | restrained text or graphic detail |
| Paper | `#FFFFFF` | product and clean surfaces |
| Paper Warm | `#F7F5F2` | preferred neutral background |
| Paper Sage | `#EEF1EA` | occasional quiet secondary background |
| Clay | `#D6A498` | small accent only |

The photographs should carry the main color. Do not build a colorful decorative background around them.

### Preferred surfaces

- pale natural wood,
- warm off-white paper,
- soft neutral linen,
- matte warm stone,
- uncluttered desk surface,
- clear glass only when demonstrating Translucent material.

### Lighting

- soft diffused window light,
- broad, gentle shadow transition,
- neutral-to-warm white balance,
- believable contact shadows,
- no dramatic spotlight,
- no hard commercial gradient,
- no exaggerated rim light.

### Camera language

- top-down for product truth and sheet layout,
- near-front for handheld scale,
- 30–45 degree editorial angle for lifestyle,
- macro only for real cut-line evidence,
- normal focal length with minimal distortion,
- enough depth of field to keep the product readable.

### Props

Preferred:

- one neutral planner,
- one simple phone or laptop,
- a plain envelope,
- a neutral pen,
- a small section of linen,
- a simple tumbler or water bottle.

Limit:

- generally zero to two props,
- no branded devices or visible third-party logos,
- no props overlapping the main sheet.

## 7. Competitor Learnings

Competitor references are for merchandising structure only.

### CustomLanyard

Useful lesson:

- large, clear product cutout,
- shape, size, material, and finish are visually separated,
- product is immediately understandable.

Do not copy:

- dense B2B configurator feeling,
- competitor branding,
- page layout,
- labels or icons.

### Zazzle

Useful lesson:

- one sticker concept is shown in multiple use contexts,
- application examples help customers imagine ownership.

Do not copy:

- marketplace-style overload,
- excessive surface variations,
- competitor compositions,
- product art.

### Stickoda

Useful lesson:

- show the full A5 sheet,
- show scale and dimensions clearly,
- make “what you receive” obvious.

Do not copy:

- bright, crowded label treatment,
- competitor visual identity,
- product artwork.

### Everstory synthesis

Everstory should combine:

- CustomLanyard’s product clarity,
- Zazzle’s selective real-life context,
- Stickoda’s sheet and scale proof,
- Everstory’s warmer, quieter, handmade editorial identity.

## 8. Core Ecommerce Image System

The final product gallery should prioritize actual product proof.

| Order | Image | Purpose | Concept Mode |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | A5 sheet held in hand | hero, scale, warmth | Allowed as provisional |
| 2 | full sheet top-down | what the customer receives | Allowed as provisional |
| 3 | die-cut edge macro | production quality proof | Wait for real product |
| 4 | original photo to finished sticker | customization proof | Wait for real product and authorized photo |
| 5 | laptop, tumbler, planner, or phone use | application context | Allowed as provisional |

Recommended final balance:

- approximately 70% real product photography,
- approximately 30% lifestyle or mockup imagery.

Before real product images exist, this ratio does not apply; all generated outputs remain concept references.

## 9. Output Ratios

| Use | Ratio |
|---|---|
| Shopify product gallery | 1:1 |
| Photo-quality example | 4:5 |
| Size or material comparison | 16:9 |
| A5 sheet-only illustration | 148:210 |
| Packaging example | 4:3 |
| Home hero | 16:9 or 21:9 |
| Collection thumbnail | 1:1 |

When uncertain, use 1:1 for PDP images and preserve crop room around the product.

## 10. Approval Levels

### Golden Style Reference

Approves:

- scene,
- composition,
- background,
- lighting,
- color treatment,
- prop restraint,
- camera language.

Does not approve:

- product accuracy,
- sticker art,
- sticker count,
- cut lines,
- material behavior.

### Golden Product Reference

Requires an actual Everstory product image and approves:

- product geometry,
- sheet proportion,
- layout,
- sticker count,
- cut shape,
- customer image identity,
- material behavior.

Never promote a generated Concept Mockup to Golden Product Reference.

## 11. Consistency Workflow

1. Select a template.
2. Fill `MOCKUP_REQUEST_TEMPLATE.json`.
3. Run preflight.
4. Generate one primary variation.
5. Score with `QA_RUBRIC.json`.
6. Change only one weak variable.
7. Approve the result as Golden Style if it passes.
8. Reuse the approved image through editing.
9. When actual product references arrive, replace the product area.
10. Re-score for product fidelity before ecommerce use.
