## `EVS_TOPDOWN_01` — Full A5 Sheet

### Purpose

- establish the default background and lighting,
- show the complete sheet,
- create the easiest scene for future product replacement.

### Default scene

- top-down camera,
- Paper Warm `#F7F5F2` or pale natural wood,
- full A5 sheet occupying 60–70% of the frame,
- zero to two restrained props,
- complete sheet boundary visible,
- gentle shadow under the sheet.

### Approved Golden Style variants

#### `GOLDEN_STYLE_01` — Pale Natural Wood

- pale natural wood background,
- neutral-warm white balance,
- soft upper-left lighting,
- gentle contact shadow,
- clean square composition,
- actual sheet replacement required before ecommerce use.

#### `GOLDEN_STYLE_02` — Warm Blush Paper (Lighting Override Required)

- warm blush-greige uncoated paper background,
- slightly peach-forward approved tone,
- sparse, irregular, multidirectional paper fibres,
- gentle organic tonal variation,
- neutral white sheet,
- broad, softly diffused illumination with no localized upper-left hotspot,
- close, faint contact shadow with no long or hard-edged directional cast,
- no wood grain, linen weave, canvas texture, crosshatch, grid, or repeated digital pattern,
- actual sheet replacement required before ecommerce use.

When using `GOLDEN_STYLE_02`, reference the approved image for its background
colour, natural paper-fibre texture, tonal family, sheet placement, crop, and
negative-space system. Use `GOLDEN_STYLE_01` only as a secondary reference for
broad natural light diffusion and shadow softness. Do not copy its pale wood
background, wood grain, colour, or sheet geometry. Do not reinterpret the blank
sheet in either reference as an actual Everstory product.

### `GOLDEN_STYLE_02` Lighting Override

- remove the visible upper-left 45-degree hotspot and any localized bright patch,
- use large-window-through-sheer-curtain or large-softbox diffusion across the full frame,
- keep exposure transitions gradual from the centre to all four edges,
- retain only a subtle, close contact shadow with a faint lower-right falloff,
- no narrow light beam, window-shaped light, hard shadow edge, long cast shadow, or heavy vignette,
- change only light distribution, exposure balance, and shadow softness,
- preserve the warm blush-greige tone, natural fibres, white-sheet geometry, top-down crop, and negative space.

### Hard Locks

- ISO A5 portrait proportion,
- entire sheet visible,
- minimal perspective distortion,
- no prop overlap,
- no text or logo invented,
- provisional stickers must not be treated as final product art.

### Default output

- Shopify PDP,
- 1:1,
- one primary variation.

## `EVS_SINGLE_01` — Single Sticker Hero

### Purpose

- establish a reusable scene for one individual die-cut sticker,
- keep the sticker area easy to replace with an actual product image,
- create a clean ecommerce hero or secondary gallery image.

### Default scene

- one blank white die-cut placeholder only,
- top-down or near-top-down camera,
- pale natural wood matching `GOLDEN_STYLE_01`,
- placeholder occupying approximately 35–45% of the frame,
- generous clean negative space,
- soft upper-left light and a restrained contact shadow,
- zero props.

### Concept Mode restriction

The blank placeholder approves scene style only. It does not prove the actual
sticker shape, size, border width, thickness, material, laminate, print color,
cut quality, or adhesive behavior.

### Hard Locks

- exactly one sticker placeholder,
- complete outer boundary visible,
- no text, logo, URL, number, watermark, or product artwork,
- no backing sheet, packaging, additional sticker, hand, or prop,
- no peeling, curling, folding, exaggerated thickness, or heavy gloss,
- preserve a simple replacement-friendly silhouette.

### Default output

- Shopify PDP or collection image,
- 1:1,
- one primary variation.

## `EVS_HANDHELD_01` — Handheld A5 Sheet

### Purpose

- communicate scale,
- add human warmth,
- serve as a potential lead image.

### Default scene

- one natural adult hand supports the lower edge or lower corner,
- sheet faces the camera at a near-front angle,
- fingers do not cover important product areas,
- background is softly out of focus,
- soft window light from one side,
- A5 sheet remains the dominant subject.

### Approved Golden Style variant

- reference: `GOLDEN_STYLE_HANDHELD_01`,
- one natural adult hand only,
- one blank portrait A5 replacement sheet only,
- complete sheet boundary and all four corners visible,
- near-front camera angle,
- pale natural wood background,
- soft upper-left light,
- clean 1:1 composition,
- actual sheet replacement required before ecommerce use.

### Hard Locks

- believable hand anatomy,
- no extra fingers,
- no jewelry or nail art that dominates the product,
- no severe bending of the sheet,
- no crop through the A5 sheet,
- no invented logo or packaging,
- preserve enough visible sheet area for replacement,
- when replacing the sheet, mask precisely around the thumb and any visible finger overlap.

### Default output

- Shopify PDP hero,
- 1:1,
- one primary variation.

## `EVS_MATERIAL_LAYOUT_01` — Four-Material Comparison Layout

### Purpose

- establish a reusable 2×2 comparison layout,
- reserve four independent replacement areas for White Matte, Translucent, Silver, and Gold,
- keep background, spacing, lighting, and label room consistent before actual material photography exists.

### Default scene

- exactly four identical blank white placeholders,
- balanced 2×2 grid,
- pale natural wood matching the approved Golden Style family,
- equal object size and spacing,
- soft upper-left light with restrained, consistent contact shadows,
- clear space below each object for labels added later in a design tool,
- zero props and no in-image text.

### Slot order

1. top left: White Matte,
2. top right: Translucent,
3. bottom left: Silver,
4. bottom right: Gold.

### Concept Mode restriction

The placeholders approve layout style only. They do not represent or prove
actual material color, opacity, transparency, reflectivity, metallic behavior,
lamination, print quality, cut quality, or product performance.

### Hard Locks

- exactly four placeholders in a 2×2 grid,
- identical provisional size and silhouette,
- no overlap or crop,
- no text, logo, URL, label, or watermark,
- no simulated transparent, silver, gold, metallic, holographic, or reflective effect,
- equal lighting treatment across all four slots,
- preserve each slot as an independent replacement area.

### Default output

- Shopify material comparison base,
- 1:1,
- one primary layout.

## `EVS_MATERIAL_01` — Material Comparison

Blocked until actual White Matte, Translucent, Silver, and Gold samples are photographed under controlled lighting.

Do not add generated reflections, transparency, or metallic effects to the
approved layout base. Any separate generated material-effect exploration must
remain a Concept Mockup and must never be presented as product evidence.
Use `GOLDEN_STYLE_MATERIAL_01` as the approved scene base, then replace all four
placeholder areas with actual material product images in the registered slot order.
