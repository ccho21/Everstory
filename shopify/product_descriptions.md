## §Common — 모든 SKU 페이지 하단에 동일하게 붙임

### How to order

1. Choose your size and material above
2. Upload your photograph(s), tell us your name, and share any detail that matters in the order notes — a face we should not crop, a tone you prefer, a detail we should keep crisp
3. We begin work within one business day. If your photograph needs replacement (resolution too low, too blurry to cut around), we will email you first.
4. Print, cut, and ship within 2–5 business days from your order

The order notes at checkout are how we hear you. The more specific the notes, the closer the result matches what you have in mind.

### Materials

Choose from four Korean premium substrates, all finished with LAMat-AF / Oraguard lamination for waterproof durability:

- **White Matte** — soft, paper-like finish. Best for diaries, journals, and indoor packaging. The classic.
- **Pearl Grey** — warm beige-grey tone with a subtle pearl sheen. For tumblers, phone cases, and outdoor surfaces.
- **Silver** — metallic silver finish. Reflective accent for waterproof use.
- **Gold** — metallic gold finish. Reflective accent for waterproof use.

All four are waterproof, fade resistant, and laminated for outdoor and dishwasher safety (top rack, occasional).

### Sizes

Sized by the longest edge:

| Size | Inches | Millimeters | Approx. stickers per sheet |
|------|--------|-------------|----------------------------|
| XS | 0.75" | 19 mm | up to 13 |
| **S (default)** | **1"** | **25 mm** | **up to 7** |
| M | 1.25" | 32 mm | up to 5 |
| L | 1.5" | 38 mm | up to 3 |
| XL | 1.75" | 45 mm | up to 3 |
| XXL | 2.5" | 64 mm | 1 |
| Mixed | 1" / 1.25" / 1.75" combined | — | a curated mix |

Sticker count varies with photo proportion. We hand-pack each sheet to maximize the surface — leftover space is filled with bonus stickers in your selected design.

### Care & lifespan

- Indoor: 5+ years without fade
- Outdoor: 2-3 years (UV-exposed). Lamination protects the print.
- Waterproof: yes, all four materials
- Dishwasher: top rack only, occasional. Hand wash recommended.
- Surfaces: tumblers, phone cases, journals, laptops, water bottles, packaging, mirrors, glass, ceramic.

### Made in Toronto

Hand cut around every subject. Hair, ears, paws, and the tiny gaps between fingers — all done by a person, not an algorithm. Each order is reviewed, mocked up, and approved before it goes to print.

Printed on Epson ET-8550 (dye ink, fade resistant), cut on Summa D75 (industrial precision). All in Toronto, by Everstory Studio.

### Lead time & shipping

- Print + cut: 1–3 business days after your order
- Ship: same or next business day after cut
- Total: typically **2–5 business days** from order to shipped

Free shipping across Ontario via Canada Post lettermail. Local pickup available at our Toronto Studio — choose at checkout.

### A note on photos

Bring us your favorite shot. We do the rest — background removal, color correction, balance. The clearer your photo, the better we can hand-cut around the subject. We will email if a photo needs replacement before we print.
