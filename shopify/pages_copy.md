```
We make sticker sheets you do not throw away.

Everstory Studio is a small, hand-run sticker shop in Toronto. We make custom photo sticker sheets — the kind you keep in a drawer for a year and pull out when the right diary or tumbler asks for one.

WHY HAND CUT

The sticker industry runs on automation. Most large shops use AI masking to cut around your pet, your child, your face. The result is fine, until you look closely at the hair, the ears, the gap between two paws. We do that part by hand.

Every sticker sheet is hand cut by a person looking at your photograph. We trace the silhouette, fix the gaps, and rebuild the edges where the algorithm gave up. It takes longer. The result is the difference between a sticker and a keepsake.

KOREAN PREMIUM SUBSTRATES

We import our materials from Korea — inkjet labels finished with LAMat-AF and Oraguard lamination. White matte. Pearl grey. Silver. Gold. All four are waterproof, fade resistant, and laminated for long-term use indoors and outdoors.

We picked Korean substrates because the print on them looks photographic, not commercial. The blacks are darker. The whites are whiter. The colors hold for years.

SAY IT IN THE NOTES

When you place an order, you upload your photo and write a few lines about what matters — a face we should not crop, a tone you prefer, a detail we should keep crisp. Those notes are how we hear you.

We start cutting within one business day. No back-and-forth. No weeks of waiting. The next time you hear from us is when your sheet is on its way.

If your photograph genuinely cannot be cut around — too low resolution, too blurry — we will email you before we begin. Otherwise, the order notes are the conversation.

MADE IN TORONTO

We print on Epson ET-8550, cut on Summa D75, and pack by hand at our studio in Toronto. Local pickup is welcome — message us at checkout if you want to swing by.

## 2. FAQ (`/pages/faq`)

**Page title**: `Frequently Asked Questions`
**Handle**: `faq`

```
ORDERING

Q. How does ordering work?
A. Choose your SKU (Solo, Duo, Trio, or Memory Pack), pick a size and material, and upload your photo and details at checkout. We start cutting within one business day and ship within 2–5 business days. No mockup back-and-forth — the order notes are how we hear what matters to you.

Q. Can I cancel my order?
A. Yes, before we begin printing — typically within one business day of your order. Email [orders@everstory-domain.com] as soon as possible.

Q. Can I request changes after I order?
A. The order notes at checkout are where we capture your details. If you missed something, reach out before printing starts (typically within one business day) and we will fold it in if we can. After printing, changes require a new order.

PHOTOS

Q. What kind of photo should I send?
A. The clearer, the better. Bright lighting, the subject filling the frame, minimal motion blur. We handle background removal and color correction.

Q. What file formats do you accept?
A. JPG, PNG, HEIC. Up to 25 MB per file. Higher resolution is always better.

Q. Will my photo look exactly like the examples on your site?
A. Every photo is different. We do our best with the photo you upload and the details you share at checkout. The clearer your photo and the more specific your notes, the closer the result matches your expectation. We will email if your photo genuinely cannot be cut around.

Q. Can I send multiple photos?
A. Solo accepts one. Duo accepts two. Trio accepts three. Memory Pack accepts four to eight.

SIZES & MATERIALS

Q. What size should I pick?
A. S (1 inch) is the most popular — fits planners, journals, water bottles, phone cases. M and L are great for laptops and tumblers. XL and XXL are statement pieces. XS is for collectors who want many small stickers. Mixed gives you a curated combination.

Q. What is the difference between the materials?
A. White matte is paper-like and best for indoor use (diaries, packaging). Pearl grey, silver, and gold are waterproof with a slight metallic finish — best for tumblers, phone cases, outdoor surfaces.

Q. Are the stickers waterproof?
A. All four materials are laminated and waterproof. Top-rack dishwasher safe occasionally; hand wash recommended for longest lifespan.

SHIPPING

Q. Where do you ship?
A. Anywhere in Ontario. Free shipping by Canada Post lettermail.

Q. How long does shipping take?
A. Lettermail typically arrives 3–7 business days after shipping confirmation. Total time from order to delivery is usually 5–12 business days.

Q. Why no tracking?
A. Lettermail is the most affordable way to ship sticker sheets. We pass that affordability on to you (free shipping). The trade-off is no tracking. If your order is lost, we reprint and reship at no cost.

Q. Can I pick up locally?
A. Yes — choose Toronto Studio Pickup at checkout. We email when your order is ready.

Q. Do you ship outside Ontario?
A. Not yet. Message us if you want to order from elsewhere — we evaluate case by case.

QUALITY & RETURNS

Q. What if my sticker has a defect?
A. We reprint and reship at no cost. Email a photo of the issue within 7 days of delivery.

Q. What if I do not like how it turned out?
A. The order notes at checkout are your moment — share anything specific that matters (face we should not crop, tone, details). After printing, we do not refund based on personal preference, but we always reprint genuine print or cut defects.

Q. How long do the stickers last?
A. Indoor: 5+ years. Outdoor (UV-exposed): 2-3 years. Lamination protects the print.

A. 네. 결제는 영어로 진행되지만, 주문 노트에 한국어로 디테일 적어주시거나 이메일로 한국어 회신 주시면 한국어로 응대합니다.

Q. Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?
A. For orders of 20+ sheets, message us at [orders@everstory-domain.com] for a custom quote.

Q. Do you make stickers other than photos?
A. Photo sticker sheets are our focus right now. Calligraphy, name tags, and logo stickers are on our roadmap. Stay subscribed for updates.

## 3. Sizing Guide (`/pages/sizing-guide`)

**Page title**: `Sticker Size Guide`
**Handle**: `sizing-guide`

```
Sticker size is measured by the longest edge of each sticker. Sheet size is fixed at A5 (148 × 195 mm). The number of stickers per sheet depends on the size you choose and the proportion of your photograph.

WHAT EACH SIZE LOOKS LIKE

XS — 0.75 inch (19 mm)
A small thumbnail size. Up to 13 stickers per sheet. Best for collectors, journal corners, calendar marks. Fine details may be hard to read at this size.

S — 1 inch (25 mm) — most popular
The default for a reason. Up to 7 stickers per sheet. Fits planners, water bottles, phone cases, laptops without overwhelming the surface.

M — 1.25 inch (32 mm)
Up to 5 stickers per sheet. A common size for tumblers and notebook covers. Good balance between presence and quantity.

L — 1.5 inch (38 mm)
Up to 3 stickers per sheet. Statement size for a single tumbler or laptop. The face or pet is clearly recognizable from across a room.

XL — 1.75 inch (45 mm)
Up to 3 stickers per sheet. Bold. Best for a single hero placement.

XXL — 2.5 inch (64 mm)
One sticker per sheet. The full statement piece. Best for car decals, large tumblers, gift packaging accents.

MIXED
A curated combination of 1.75 / 1.5 / 1.25 / 1 inch sizes on a single sheet. Best for variety — different uses, different surfaces.

SIZE BY USE CASE

- Phone case: S or M
- Water bottle: S, M, or L
- Laptop: M or L
- Tumbler (16-30 oz): M or L
- Journal / planner: XS or S
- Car decal: XL or XXL
- Gift packaging: S or M
- Calendar: XS

PROPORTION NOTE

The "longest edge" measurement means a horizontal photograph and a vertical photograph at the same size letter (S, M, etc.) will be the same width — but the vertical photograph will be taller. Sticker count adjusts automatically to fit.

If you are unsure, S (1 inch) is the safe choice. We ship more S stickers than every other size combined.
```

## 4. Materials Guide (`/pages/materials-guide`)

**Page title**: `Material Guide`
**Handle**: `materials-guide`

```
We use four Korean premium substrates, all finished with LAMat-AF or Oraguard lamination. Pick the material that matches the surface and the use.

WHITE MATTE

Soft, paper-like finish with no shine. The classic photo sticker look — neutral, quiet, lets your image lead.

Best for:
- Diaries, planners, journals
- Indoor packaging, gift boxes
- Calendar marks, mood boards
- Anywhere you want the photo to read clearly without competing surface effects

Indoor lifespan: 5+ years
Outdoor lifespan: 2-3 years
Waterproof: yes
Dishwasher: top rack only, occasional

PEARL GREY

Warm beige-grey base with a subtle pearl sheen. The most editorial of our four — looks like a fine paper sample, not a sticker.

Best for:
- Tumblers, water bottles
- Phone cases (works on every case color)
- Mirror surfaces, glass
- Outdoor packaging where weather matters
- Photographs with warm tones (skin, wood, golden hour)

Indoor lifespan: 5+ years
Outdoor lifespan: 2-3 years
Waterproof: yes
Dishwasher: top rack only, occasional

SILVER

Metallic silver substrate. Reflective, clean, modern.

Best for:
- Tumblers and water bottles where you want metallic accents
- Cool-toned photographs (winter, monochrome, modern interiors)
- Logo or text stickers paired with photo sheets
- Gift packaging where you want shine

Indoor lifespan: 5+ years
Outdoor lifespan: 2-3 years
Waterproof: yes
Dishwasher: top rack only, occasional

GOLD

Metallic gold substrate. Warm, luxurious, classic.

Best for:
- Special occasions — wedding, anniversary, holiday gift
- Warm-toned photographs (sunset, candlelight, golden hour)
- Tumblers and packaging that need a celebratory accent
- Pet portraits where you want a vintage frame feel

Indoor lifespan: 5+ years
Outdoor lifespan: 2-3 years
Waterproof: yes
Dishwasher: top rack only, occasional

CARE FOR ALL FOUR MATERIALS

- Apply to clean, dry surfaces
- Press firmly across the entire sticker, edges first
- Wait 24 hours before exposing to water if possible
- Hand wash for longest lifespan
- Avoid acetone, harsh solvents, abrasive scrubbing

REMOVAL

Stickers peel cleanly from most surfaces (glass, metal, plastic) within the first year. After 1–2 years on a heavily used surface (tumblers, water bottles), residue may remain — a drop of citrus oil or rubbing alcohol clears it.

Not recommended for: porous wood, fabric, anything you cannot clean.

WHICH ONE FOR YOU

- Indoor only, paper-like look → White Matte
- Outdoor or wet surfaces, warm tone → Pearl Grey
- Cool / modern aesthetic, metallic accent → Silver
- Special occasion, warm celebration → Gold

Still unsure? Order Solo with White Matte first. Once you see how a sheet feels in your hand, the right material for your next order picks itself.
```
