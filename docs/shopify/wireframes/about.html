Made in Toronto

Made to keep, in Toronto.

A small, hand-run sticker shop. We make custom photo sticker sheets — the kind you keep in a drawer for a year and pull out when the right diary or tumbler asks for one.

HAND CUTTING · CLOSE-UP
01 — Why hand cut

By a person, not an algorithm.

The sticker industry runs on automation. Most large shops use AI masking to cut around your pet, your child, your face. The result is fine, until you look closely at the hair, the ears, the gap between two paws.

Every sticker sheet is hand cut by a person looking at your photograph. We trace the silhouette, fix the gaps, and rebuild the edges where the algorithm gave up. It takes longer. The result is the difference between a sticker and a keepsake.

02 — Korean substrates

The materials we trust.

We import our materials from Korea — inkjet labels finished with LAMat-AF and Oraguard lamination. White matte. Pearl grey. Silver. Gold. All four are waterproof, fade resistant, and laminated for long-term use indoors and outdoors.

We picked Korean substrates because the print on them looks photographic, not commercial. The blacks are darker. The whites are whiter. The colors hold for years.

ORDER NOTES · DETAIL
03 — Say it in the notes

The order notes are the conversation.

When you place an order, you upload your photo and write a few lines about what matters — a face we should not crop, a tone you prefer, a detail we should keep crisp. Those notes are how we hear you.

We start cutting within one business day. No back-and-forth. No weeks of waiting. The next time you hear from us is when your sheet is on its way.

04 — Made in Toronto

Printed, cut, packed by hand.

We print on Epson ET-8550, cut on Summa D75, and pack by hand at our studio in Toronto. Local pickup is welcome — message us at checkout if you want to swing by.
