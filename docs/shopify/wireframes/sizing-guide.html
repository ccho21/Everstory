Sticker Size Guide

Sticker size is measured by the longest edge. Sheet size is fixed at A5 (148 × 195 mm). Sticker count per sheet adjusts to your photo's proportion.

XS

XS — 0.75 in (19 mm)

Up to 13 stickers / sheet

Thumbnail size. Collectors, journal corners, calendar marks. Fine details may be hard to read.

Most popular
S

S — 1 in (25 mm)

Up to 7 stickers / sheet

Default for a reason. Planners, water bottles, phone cases, laptops without overwhelming the surface.

M

M — 1.25 in (32 mm)

Up to 5 stickers / sheet

Common for tumblers and notebook covers. Good balance between presence and quantity.

L

L — 1.5 in (38 mm)

Up to 3 stickers / sheet

Statement size for a single tumbler or laptop. Recognizable from across a room.

XL

XL — 1.75 in (45 mm)

Up to 3 stickers / sheet

Bold. Best for a single hero placement.

XXL

XXL — 2.5 in (64 mm)

1 sticker / sheet

Full statement piece. Car decals, large tumblers, gift packaging accents.

MIX

Mixed

Variety pack

A curated combination of 1.75 / 1.5 / 1.25 / 1 inch sizes on a single sheet. Best when you want the same photo at different uses and surfaces.

A note on proportion

The "longest edge" measurement means a horizontal photo and a vertical photo at the same size letter (S, M, etc.) will be the same width — but the vertical photo will be taller. Sticker count per sheet adjusts automatically to fit.

If you're unsure, S (1 inch) is the safe choice. We ship more S stickers than every other size combined.
