Frequently Asked

Ordering, photos, sizes, materials, shipping, returns. Still curious? Email us.

Ordering

How does ordering work?
Choose your SKU (Solo, Duo, Trio, or Memory Pack), pick a size and material, and upload your photo and details at checkout. We start cutting within one business day and ship within 2–5 business days. No mockup back-and-forth — the order notes are how we hear what matters to you.
Can I cancel my order?
Yes, before we begin printing — typically within one business day of your order. Email orders@everstory-domain.com as soon as possible.
Can I request changes after I order?
The order notes at checkout are where we capture your details. If you missed something, reach out before printing starts (typically within one business day) and we will fold it in if we can. After printing, changes require a new order.

Photos

What kind of photo should I send?
The clearer, the better. Bright lighting, the subject filling the frame, minimal motion blur. We handle background removal and color correction.
What file formats do you accept?
JPG, PNG, HEIC. Up to 25 MB per file. Higher resolution is always better.
Will my photo look exactly like the examples on your site?
Every photo is different. We do our best with the photo you upload and the details you share at checkout. The clearer your photo and the more specific your notes, the closer the result matches your expectation.
Can I send multiple photos?
Solo accepts one. Duo accepts two. Trio accepts three. Memory Pack accepts four to eight.

Sizes & materials

What size should I pick?
S (1 inch) is the most popular — fits planners, journals, water bottles, phone cases. M and L are great for laptops and tumblers. XL and XXL are statement pieces. XS is for collectors. Mixed gives you a curated combination.
What is the difference between the materials?
White matte is paper-like and best for indoor use. Pearl grey, silver, and gold are waterproof with a slight metallic finish — best for tumblers, phone cases, outdoor surfaces.
Are the stickers waterproof?
All four materials are laminated and waterproof. Top-rack dishwasher safe occasionally; hand wash recommended.

Shipping

Where do you ship?
Anywhere in Ontario. Free shipping by Canada Post lettermail.
How long does shipping take?
Lettermail typically arrives 3–7 business days after shipping confirmation. Total time from order to delivery is usually 5–12 business days.
Why no tracking?
Lettermail is the most affordable way to ship sticker sheets. Free shipping is the trade-off. If your order is lost, we reprint and reship at no cost.
Can I pick up locally?
Yes — choose Toronto Studio Pickup at checkout.
Do you ship outside Ontario?
Not yet. Message us — we evaluate case by case.

Quality & returns

What if my sticker has a defect?
We reprint and reship at no cost. Email a photo of the issue within 7 days of delivery.
What if I do not like how it turned out?
After printing, we do not refund based on personal preference, but we always reprint genuine print or cut defects.
How long do the stickers last?
Indoor: 5+ years. Outdoor (UV-exposed): 2-3 years.

Other

Can I order in Korean?
네. 결제는 영어로 진행되지만, 주문 노트에 한국어로 디테일 적어주시거나 이메일로 한국어 회신 주시면 한국어로 응대합니다.
Do you offer wholesale or bulk pricing?
For orders of 20+ sheets, message us at orders@everstory-domain.com for a custom quote.
Do you make stickers other than photos?
Photo sticker sheets are our focus right now. Calligraphy, name tags, and logo stickers are on our roadmap.

한국어 자주 묻는 질문

주문은 어떻게 진행되나요?
상품 (Solo / Duo / Trio / Memory Pack) 과 사이즈, 재질을 선택하고 결제 시 사진과 함께 주문 노트에 원하는 디테일을 적어주세요. 1영업일 안에 작업을 시작해서 2–5영업일 안에 배송됩니다.
한국어로 주문 가능한가요?
결제는 영어로 진행되지만, 주문 노트에 한국어로 디테일 적어주시거나 이메일로 한국어 회신 주시면 한국어로 응대합니다.
한국으로 배송되나요?
현재는 캐나다 온타리오주 내에서만 배송됩니다. 한국 또는 미국 발송이 필요한 경우 이메일로 별도 문의 주세요.
토론토에서 직접 픽업할 수 있나요?
네, 가능합니다. 결제 시 "Toronto Studio Pickup" 선택. 준비 완료 시 이메일로 안내드립니다.
사진은 어떤 것이 좋은가요?
밝은 자연광, 피사체가 화면을 가득 채우고, 흔들림이 적은 사진이 좋습니다. 배경 제거와 색감 보정은 저희가 진행합니다.
환불은 어떻게 되나요?
인쇄 시작 전까지 (보통 1영업일 이내) 전액 환불 가능. 인쇄 결함은 100% 무상 재제작, 배송 분실 (14영업일 미수령) 도 1회 무상 재제작 진행합니다.
사이즈는 어떻게 선택하나요?
S (1인치, 25mm) 가 가장 인기. 다이어리·플래너·텀블러·핸드폰 케이스에 무난. 고민되시면 S 추천.
