Before you order

A few notes.

  • Every sheet is made to order. Once production begins, we can't refund a change of mind.
  • The photo and order notes you submit at checkout are what we work from. We proceed from your notes, with no separate proof step.
  • Please send a sharp, high-resolution photo. If a photo is too blurry or low quality, we may ask for a replacement before we start.
  • Printing and cutting take 1–3 business days, and we ship the same or next day.
  • From order to shipment usually takes 2–5 business days.
  • Printing and cutting can vary by about 0.2–2 mm.
  • Screen color and printed color can differ. See the color note below.
  • The number of stickers can change with the photo's ratio.
  • Free shipping across Ontario (Canada Post lettermail). Toronto studio pickup is also available.
  • Questions? Email orders@everstory-domain.com. We're happy to help in English or Korean.
