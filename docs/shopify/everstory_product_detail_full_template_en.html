Before you order

Before you order.

  • All sheets are made to order. Refunds for change of mind are not available once production starts.
  • Your uploaded photo and order notes form the brief. We work directly from the notes — no separate proof step.
  • Sharper, higher-resolution photos give cleaner results.
  • Print and cut take 1–3 business days. Shipping starts the same or next business day.
  • Total time from order to shipped is typically 2–5 business days.
  • Print and cut have a 0.2–2 mm tolerance for shift or alignment.
  • Screen color and printed color may differ. See the color note below.
  • Sticker count may vary with photo proportion.
  • Free shipping across Ontario via Canada Post lettermail. Toronto Studio pickup is also available.
  • Questions: orders@everstory-domain.com. Korean and English supported.
